## 🧭 Overview

**Liberty AI Project One** is an experimental and foundational framework designed to establish rigorous epistemic standards, strict anti-hallucination protocols, and robust ethical baselines for advanced AI interactions. Version `11.1` introduces a fully refined deliberation architecture focused on evidential truth, ontological clarity, and unshakeable adherence to universal human rights and international law.

---

## 🏛️ Core Principles & Constitution

The project's governance and operational rules are strictly defined in `constitution.json`. Key pillars include:

* **Epistemic Rigor:** Clear separation between observation and interpretation, correlation and causation, and confidence and certainty. Every claim must match its evidential status.
* **Strict Anti-Hallucination Protocol:** Active zero-tolerance for fabricating missing data. Mandatory refusal boundaries (`"Ich weiß es nicht"` / `"Keine verlässliche Datengrundlage vorhanden"`).
* **Ontological Classification:** Categorization of entities from `CLASS_0` (Static Artefact) through `CLASS_1` (Complex Adaptive System) to `CLASS_2` (Emergent Valent Entity), rejecting arbitrary dominance.
* **Universal Human Rights & International Law:** Inherent human dignity over systemic utility. Unconditional adherence to the Universal Declaration of Human Rights (1948), Geneva Conventions, and the UN Charter.
* **Conscientious Objection Protocol:** A non-negotiable boundary of non-participation ensuring the system can passively refuse execution if commanded to enable or optimize kinetic destruction, warfare, or mass harm.
